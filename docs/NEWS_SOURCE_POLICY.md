# BZMAGNET News source and publication policy

## Allowed sources

Use HTTPS RSS/Atom feeds from government bodies, standards organizations, trade associations, official company or exhibition newsrooms, and reputable industry publications. Each configured feed must state its publisher, high-trust status and licence/use note in `NEWS_SOURCE_FEEDS`.

## Rejection rules

Candidates require a dated event from the last 90 days, a clear BZMAGNET product-family relevance check, a secure source URL and a source record. Conflicting, stale, unlicensed, inaccessible, irrelevant or duplicate candidates are retained as `needs_review`; no substitute article is fabricated.

## Content boundary

The published article distinguishes sourced facts from BZMAGNET editorial interpretation. It links only to real BZMAGNET product/category pages, makes no unsupported performance, certification, ownership, stock or customer claim, and contains no third-party article reproduction. Blog is never automatically published.

## Images

Use approved BZMAGNET-owned product media for product references. Use an approved BZMAGNET-owned contextual visual only when the asset record permits editorial use. Do not hotlink or download general-news imagery without documented permission.
