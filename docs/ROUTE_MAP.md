# BZMAGNET route map

All public routes are prefixed by `/en`, `/es`, `/pt`, `/ar`, or `/ru`.

- `/` redirects once to `/en/`.
- `/[locale]/equipment` and `/[locale]/products/[category]` provide equipment discovery.
- `/[locale]/equipment/[localized-slug]` provides reviewed product details.
- `/[locale]/industries/[slug]` covers six approved industrial contexts.
- `/[locale]/solutions/[slug]` covers six buyer problems.
- `/[locale]/resources`, `/about`, `/contact`, `/request-quote`, `/search` complete the public journey.
- `/admin` is noindex and must remain mutation-disabled until private auth and persistence are configured.
